# About Us

**Welcome to Jindal Panther Cement -**  a leader in eco-friendly, high-performance cement solutions that prioritise both innovation and sustainability. Our range of Composite Cement, Portland Slag Cement, and Ground-granulated Blast-furnace Slag (GGBS) cement is designed to meet the highest standards of durability and strength. Using a perfect blend of superior clinker, superfine fly ash, and highly reactive slag and silica, Panther Cement delivers unmatched quality, making it ideal for load-bearing structures like beams, columns, slabs, and foundations.

Our commitment to environmental responsibility and excellence is embodied in every product we produce. With a **1 MTPA** facility currently operational in Raigarh, Chattisgarh, and a new **1.5 MTPA** facility underway in Angul, Odisha, Jindal Panther Cement is ready to support a sustainable future for construction. Discover Panther Cement's dedication to strength, durability, and eco-friendly innovation, and build with a partner you can trust..

## Composite Cement

Jindal Panther Composite Cement is a versatile and high-performance product that combines the advantages of both Ordinary Portland Cement (OPC) and pozzolanic materials. This results in enhanced durability, reduced permeability, and improved workability for various construction applications.

#### **Advantages**

* **High Durability:** Ideal choice for robust and reliable construction.
* **Lower Heat of Hydration:** Reduces the risk of thermal cracking.
* **Improved Workability:** Smoother application, easier handling, and a consistent mix.
* **Environmentally Friendly:** Minimises the overall environmental footprint.

# Ground-granulated Blast-furnace Slag

## Ground-Granulated Blast-Furnace Slag

Jindal Panther Ground-Granulated Blast-Furnace Slag is a supplementary cementitious material that enhances the performance of concrete. It is produced by quenching molten iron slag from a blast furnace with water and then processing it into a fine powder.

#### **Advantages**

* **High Compressive Strength:** Improves the overall strength and load-bearing capacity of concrete.
* **Lower Heat Evolution:** Reduces the heat generated during the hydration process, preventing thermal cracking.
* **Improved Durability:** Enhances resistance to chemical attacks and external weathering.
* **Sustainable Solution:** Utilizes industrial by-products, promoting responsible waste management.

## Panther Portland Slag Cement

Jindal Panther Portland Slag Cement is a blend of OPC and granulated blast-furnace slag, offering superior performance in terms of strength, durability, and sustainability. This product is suitable for a wide range of construction projects, providing excellent value for builders.

#### **Advantages**

* **Enhanced Strength:** Achieves higher compressive strength compared to traditional Portland cement.
* **Reduced Permeability:** Improves resistance to water penetration, enhancing durability.
* **Lower Heat Evolution:** Mitigates the risk of thermal cracking during the curing process.
* **Sustainable Choice:** Incorporates industrial by-products, contributing to eco-friendly construction.

# Products Overview

Jindal Panther Composite Cement is a versatile and high-performance product that combines the advantages of both Ordinary Portland Cement (OPC) and pozzolanic materials. This results in enhanced durability, reduced permeability, and improved workability for various construction applications.

![](https://ppel.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/jindalcement/custom/1731483276products-overview-composite-cement.jpg)

### Composite Cement

Jindal Panther's Composite Cement is a versatile solution that combines the benefits of both Portland cement and pozzolanic materials. This product offers enhanced durability and strength, making it an ideal choice for a wide range of construction projects. With excellent workability and reduced heat of hydration, Composite Cement ensures sustainable and resilient structures.

![](https://ppel.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/jindalcement/custom/1731483518products-overview-portland-slag-cement.jpg)

### Portland Slag Cement

Jindal Panther's Portland Slag Cement is a blend of clinker, gypsum, and granulated blast furnace slag. This product is known for its superior compressive strength and durability, making it suitable for various applications, including residential, commercial, and infrastructure projects. It not only enhances the  
long-term performance of concrete but also contributes to sustainable construction practices.

![](https://ppel.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/jindalcement/custom/1731482583products-overview-ggbs.jpg)

### GGBS

Jindal Panther's Ground-Granulated Blast-Furnace Slag (GGBS) is a byproduct of the iron and steel manufacturing process.  
This finely ground slag is used as a supplementary cementitious material, improving the workability, durability, and chemical resistance of concrete. GGBS is an environmentally friendly option that helps reduce the carbon footprint of construction projects, making it an essential choice for sustainable building practices.

![](https://ppel.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/107/1760523151_products-overview-tuffy-cement.jpg)

### Tuffy Cement

Jindal Panther Tuffy is a high-quality Composite Cement (PCC) in a convenient 5 kg pack, offering trusted strength, durability, and excellent workability. Ideal for small-scale projects, it combines superior performance with easy handling and reliability.

### GGBS

Jindal Panther's Ground-Granulated Blast-Furnace Slag (GGBFS) is a byproduct of the iron and steel manufacturing process. This finely ground slag is used as a supplementary cementitious material, improving the workability, durability, and chemical resistance of concrete. GGBFS is an environmentally friendly option that helps reduce the carbon footprint of construction projects, making it an essential choice for sustainable building practices.
